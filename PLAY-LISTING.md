## Full description (4000 max)
Trying to swear less? Make it a game.

PottyMouthPocket is the swear jar with a difference. Start a jar with your house, your friends, your family or your team. When you hear someone swear, tap "I heard a swear!" and accuse them. They can hold their hands up, or dispute it and let the rest of the group vote. At the end of the round, the cleanest mouth wins.

YOU CHOOSE WHAT'S AT STAKE
• Bragging rights: no money, just glory
• A forfeit: the dirtiest mouth buys the takeaway, does the dishes, whatever you agree
• Money: set your own prices for a mild slip, a proper swear and absolute filth. The app keeps the tally; your group pays into a shared wallet, a real jar, or settles up at the end

FAIR PLAY BUILT IN
• Accept or dispute every accusation
• The group votes on disputes, so nobody gets stitched up
• A full ledger of who said what, and when

KEEP SCORE TOGETHER
• Live leaderboard: who's the cleanest, and who's filling the jar
• Countdown to the end of the round
• Share the winner's result card with the group
• Nudge someone on WhatsApp when they've been caught

SIMPLE AND PRIVATE
• No email or password needed: just type your name
• Invite people with a link or a 6-letter code
• Only people in your jar can see what happens in it
• No adverts

PottyMouthPocket never takes, holds or sends money. It's a scorekeeper; any money is arranged between your group, outside the app.
